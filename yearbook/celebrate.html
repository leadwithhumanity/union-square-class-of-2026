Celebrate With Us

You are part of our story

You're receiving this because you are an important part of our story. Maybe you stayed with us, cleaned up the West Side with us, served on a board with us, hung a wall, shared a meal, or simply made this place feel like home. Twenty years in this old gym were built by people like you.

Why we gather

We believe the moments that change us deserve to be marked on purpose, not slipped past. A home that held this much love shouldn't just be emptied and handed over. So we're doing it the way we've tried to do everything here: together. We'll remember what this place gave us, thank it, let it go with gratitude, and bless it for whoever comes next. Then we'll watch the sun go down in the same window where it all began.

One gray bird landed the day Erica found Union Square. Now two of us are taking flight. We'd love for you to be there.
