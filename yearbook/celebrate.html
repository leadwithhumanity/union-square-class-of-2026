Celebrate With Us

Graduation weekend

Two gatherings at 224 before the keys change hands: a Friday evening with the people who made this place what it was, and a Saturday afternoon to walk the gym one last time. Everyone who ever stayed, cleaned up, hung a wall or helped is invited. RSVP below so we know who's coming to which.

23Friday, October

Graduation Gathering

5 to 8 pm at 224, with a sunset toast on the rooftop deck around 6:45

We close on the loft at noon that day. By five, the gym is ours for one more evening. Come for the whole thing or for the toast.

  • Charcuterie and Fratelli's pizza; bring nothing but a memory
  • Sunset toast to the starlight and the window, weather permitting on the roof, otherwise under the window
  • The guest journals, out on the butcher block for one last signing, and guest-book hour at 6
  • Class photo under the window at the toast, with the starlight on

24Saturday, October

Open Gym: the last look

2 to 5 pm, drop in

A slower, daylight version for neighbors, former guests, kids and anyone who could not make Friday. Walk the three floors, stand at the window, find your picture in the yearbook, sign it.

  • Walk the three floors one last time; record your Loft story upstairs if you like
  • Find yourself: the class photos on the big screen; tag your picture and leave a note
  • Estate sale pickups: anything you bought, or still want, goes home with you today
  • Kids welcome; the rooftop pool is closed for the season

Ways to take part

Nothing to buy and nothing to prepare. Pick the ones that speak to you; they are built so that anyone can join in the moment they arrive.

Friday and Saturday

Sign the yearbook

The four guest journals on the butcher block, plus the online Autographs page by QR. Both go into the record.

Friday, sunset

The starlight toast

When the sun drops behind the skyline, one toast under the starlight, then one sentence from anyone who wants one: what this place was to you.

Friday, right after the toast

Class photo under the window

Everyone in front of the 24-foot window with the starlight lit, while there's still light. The last page of the yearbook.

Friday, 6 to 6:30

Guest-book hour

The journals open to random pages. Read a stranger's entry aloud, thirty seconds each, and hear what twelve years of guests said about this room.

Both days

Superlatives

Ballots for "Most likely to..." for Brian and Erica, counted and read aloud at the toast. Winning entries go on the Senior Portraits page.

Both days, third floor

Record your Loft story

A phone on a tripod in the quiet room upstairs. One minute, your story of this place, if you want to tell it. They become a short film in the yearbook.

Give where we gave

No gifts, but

If you want to bring something, bring a gift for one of the twenty organizations on the Give page, in honor of the Loft. A card by the door lists them.
