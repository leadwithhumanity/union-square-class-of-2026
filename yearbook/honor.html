Service and giving

From 2010 to 2026, a percentage of every stay at the Loft went to local nonprofits. This is where it went, and where we put our time.

Brian and Erica, together

Every stay

we shared a percentage of our profits with local nonprofits, from 2010 to 2026. Among the first were Well House, Steepletown Neighborhood Services and the HQ drop-in center for runaway and homeless youth (now AYA Youth Collective). We also gifted nights to nonprofits for respite and for fundraising auctions.

Where the estate sale proceeds go

The West Side Community Clean Up

In 2009, while we were dating, we started picking up trash on our block around Union Square and invited friends to join us. The first Clean Up had 15 volunteers; today it draws over 1,000, carried on by the West Grand Neighborhood Organization and now also called the City Wide Clean Up. All proceeds from our estate sale go to support it, to fund a community service project next spring that brings the neighborhood's next generation together. Watch for the invitation; we'll be back for it.
