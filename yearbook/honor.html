Service and giving

From 2009 to 2026, a percentage of every stay at the Loft went to local nonprofits. This is where it went, and where we put our time.

Every stay

we shared a percentage of our profits with local nonprofits, from 2009 to 2026. Among the first were Well House, Steepletown Neighborhood Services and the HQ drop-in center for runaway and homeless youth (now AYA Youth Collective). We also gifted nights to nonprofits for respite and for fundraising auctions.

Where the estate sale proceeds go

The West Side Community Clean Up

In 2009, while we were dating, we started picking up trash on our block around Union Square and invited friends to join us. The first Clean Up had 15 volunteers; today it draws over 1,000, carried on by the West Grand Neighborhood Organization and now also called the City Wide Clean Up. All proceeds from our estate sale go to support it, to fund a community service project next spring that brings the neighborhood's next generation together. Watch for the invitation; we'll be back for it.

Organizations we supported, 2009 to 2026

Where the Loft gave

Our joint purpose has always been united in our love of people and in sharing our time, talents and treasure with organizations we believe in, ones doing vital work for our community. Giving back is an intentional part of our business model: donating time, money and nights to these organizations, and supporting local businesses (that shout‑out list is coming next). Over 17 years, that adds up to 78 organizations, so far.

No gifts, just your presence. And if you feel inspired, we invite you to consider these extraordinary organizations. Tap a group to see them.
