Erica

What's next

More of the lake, more of the people she loves, and two passports in progress.

  • Keep helping nonprofits put AI to good use
  • Host retreats at the lake
  • Become a citizen of Germany and Estonia
  • More time with family
  • Keep the door open, now at the lakeshore

One gray bird landed the day Erica found this place. Twenty years later, two of us are taking flight.

Pigeons mate for life, and homing pigeons find their way home better together. Lake Michigan is home base now. The door is still open; it just has a bigger window.

The Next Chapter

Beachwood

In 2020 we found a cottage two blocks from Muskegon Lake and a short walk to Pere Marquette Beach. It became Beachwood Retreats: a place where friends, family and teams come to slow down, with a sunset almost every night. The hospitality we learned at the Loft lives on here.

The house

Cedar sauna, hot tub, porch, and a door that is always open.

The retreats

Nonprofit teams, leadership circles, Face It reunions, family weekends and friends who needed the lake. Six years of people finding their way back to themselves.

The lake

Lake Michigan does the rest.
