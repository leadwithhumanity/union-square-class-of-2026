Beachwood

In 2020 we found a cottage two blocks from Muskegon Lake and a short walk to Pere Marquette Beach. It became Beachwood Retreats: a place where friends, family and teams come to slow down, with a sunset almost every night. The hospitality we learned at the Loft lives on here.

The house

Cedar sauna, hot tub, porch, and a door that is always open.

The retreats

Nonprofit teams, leadership circles, Face It reunions, family weekends and friends who needed the lake. Six years of people finding their way back to themselves.

The lake

Lake Michigan does the rest.
