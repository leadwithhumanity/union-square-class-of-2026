Give where we gave

The estate sale goes to seven West Side neighbors, and these are organizations Brian and Erica have supported from the Loft. If this place or these two people meant something to you, a gift to any of them is the best thank-you we can think of.

No gift to us, please. Pick one below and give in honor of the Loft at Union Square, Class of 2026.
