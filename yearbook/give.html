Give where we gave

The estate sale goes to the West Side Community Clean Up we founded, and these are organizations Brian and Erica have supported from the Loft. If this place or these two people meant something to you, a gift to any of them is the best thank-you we can think of.

No gift to us, please. Pick one below and give in honor of the Loft at Union Square, Class of 2026.
Where the estate sale goes

Every dollar goes to the West Side Community Clean Up

In 2008, while we were dating, we started picking up trash on our block around Union Square and invited friends to join us. It became an annual spring Saturday with 200+ volunteers, now carried on by the West Grand Neighborhood Organization. Every dollar from our estate sale goes back to it, so the neighborhood that gave us so much keeps getting a little cleaner and a little closer.
