Portrait of a Place

How it felt to live here

Twelve years of phone pictures from the loft, the balcony and the rooftop. No staging, no filters. Just the light, the seasons and the old school we got to call home.

Light

The arched windows of the old gym, every hour of the day.

The building

Union High School, 1900s. Union Square, now.

Dusk

The West Side skyline, the basilica domes, and a thousand sunsets from the balcony.

The rooftop

Downtown on one side, the river on the other, a pool in between.

Seasons

Maples on fire, snow on the rail, a tulip in the window.

Photographs by Erica and Brian, 2013 to 2026.
