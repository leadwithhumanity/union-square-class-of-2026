Celebrate With Us

We're Graduating

Two lives joined in brick;
a thousand strangers came home.
Old school walls held us.

Light through the arched glass
fell on every face we loved.
Now we leave it lit.

We give thanks for light;
with open hearts, listening
for what calls us next.

You're receiving this because you are an important part of our story. Maybe you stayed with us, cleaned up the West Side with us, served on a board with us, hung a wall, shared a meal, or simply made this place feel like home. This old gym is an architectural masterpiece, and it was an honor to share it with the world. You helped us along the way.

Why we gather

“We’re all just walking each other home.”Ram Dass

We believe the moments that change us deserve to be marked on purpose, not slipped past. A home that held this much love shouldn't just be emptied and handed over. So we're doing it the way we've tried to do everything here: together. We will gather to honor our home at Union Square. We'll remember what this place gave us, thank it, and let it go with gratitude. Then we'll watch the sun go down in the same window where it all began.

One gray bird landed the day Erica found Union Square. Now two of us are taking flight. We'd love for you to be there.
