We're Graduating

Maybe you stayed, served or shared an experience with us. Thank you for helping us co-create home between these walls for the past twenty years of our lives.

Why we gather

Inspired by Priya Parker’s The Art of Gathering: we gather to celebrate, to remember, and to mark transitions. This weekend is all three.

A home that held this much love shouldn't just be emptied and handed over. So we'll do it the way we've tried to do everything here: together. We'll remember the relationships we've nurtured, the lessons we've learned and the love we've shared, and release this season with gratitude.

We'd love for you to come celebrate with us as we take flight into the next chapter.

“We’re all just walking each other home.”- Ram Dass
