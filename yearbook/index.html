Celebrating 20 Years in Community

We arrived with different stories and histories. Here we met, fell in love, got married and built our lives over the past two decades. Please celebrate with us.

Family, friends, neighbors, guests and colleagues: you're receiving this because you are an important part of our story. This old gym is an architectural masterpiece, and it was an honor to share it with the world. You helped us along the way. Before we hand over the keys, we will gather to honor this Gem in the Gym, and we want you with us.
