Welcoming travelers from all over the world

More than 1,000 guests from all over the world found their way to our little loft in the city, and we gifted nights to visiting speakers and guests of local nonprofits. We also hosted small groups from Genesis, Mars Hill and Tribes, Brian’s Cornerstone cohorts, our lifelong friends, our Leading Edge cohort, and nonprofit and philanthropy teams for team building and strategy.

Grand Rapids, 2013 to 2014

Save Airbnb in Grand Rapids

When the City Commission was set to vote on homesharing, we started a petition asking it to delay the vote and protect homesharing in Grand Rapids. More than 1,000 people signed, and we carried their names to City Hall. In November 2014, the Loft received one of the city’s first homesharing licenses.

The Homesharers Creed

Homesharers for Grand Rapids, read to the Grand Rapids Planning Commission, June 2014

  1. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is an important and emerging model that should be welcomed and incentivized in Grand Rapids.
  2. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is a low risk activity, as evidenced by the 3.5 year spotless track record that exists in Grand Rapids.
  3. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is good for our neighborhoods. It introduces new potential employers, employees and homeowners to parts of the city that they might not experience without these additional hospitality choices.
  4. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is good for our economy. Four economic impact studies have proven that Airbnb guests stay longer and spend more money in the local community.
  5. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is good for tourism and hospitality. It expands more choices by offering more spaces in more places for diverse incomes.
  6. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is good for entrepreneurialism. It provides a vehicle for citizens to earn revenue from their assets.
  7. We believe owner-occupied homesharing is good for philanthropy. It provides a vehicle for citizens to share revenue earned from their assets.
  8. We believe owner-occupied homesharing fills an important gap in the hospitality, rental and homeownership markets, and that over-regulating and criminalizing owner-occupied homesharing will make us one of the least inclusive and hospitable cities in our nation.
