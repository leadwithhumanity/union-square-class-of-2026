Our Story

Two homes, one old school

Union Square is the old Union High School. When it reopened as condos, Brian moved into Unit 224, in the old gymnasium, in 2006. Erica moved into Unit 431, in the old auditorium, in 2007.

We met here, became friends, and on New Year’s Eve 2008 went to the Union Square party together. We married in 2010, and Erica moved into the gym. Neither of us has lived anywhere longer.
