20 Years of Memories

Family blending, expansion and contraction

To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven: a time to be born, and a time to die; a time to build up; a time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance; a time to embrace; a time to keep, and a time to release.Adapted from Ecclesiastes 3

We arrived here solo. Together, with our blended family and friends, we created our home. Over twenty years we welcomed two daughters-in-law and three grandchildren. Erica found her long-lost family, and with it we welcomed new family members. We celebrated new spouses and partners for our nieces and nephews, and watched Erica’s Girl Scouts grow up and start families of their own. We also said goodbye to Brian’s mother, and to Erica’s adoptive and biological fathers.

There are thousands of pictures; these are a few, oldest to newest. We hope you find yourself in here, and that they make you smile.
