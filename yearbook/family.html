Girl Scouts

Where it started for Erica

Before the consulting, the retreats and the Loft, Erica spent the 1990s in Girl Scouts: a troop leader in Michigan, a unit leader in Frankfurt, Germany, and the founding director of the teen programs at Girl Scouts of Michigan Trails.

  • Girls and Giving, the K-12 philanthropy curriculum she wrote, launched nationally to 3.5 million girls.
  • Face It, her multigenerational mentoring program, whose women still gather 25 years later, most recently at Beachwood in 2026.
